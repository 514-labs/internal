# Getting Started

Welcome to the team! This guide will help you navigate your first few days and weeks.

## Before Your First Day

- [ ] Accept your offer letter
- [ ] Complete background check
- [ ] Set up your bank account for direct deposit
- [ ] Review the pre-boarding materials sent to your email

## Your First Day

1. **Check your email** - You'll receive login credentials and calendar invites
2. **Join the all-hands meeting** - Meet the whole team
3. **Set up your workstation** - Follow the IT setup guide
4. **Meet your buddy** - They'll help you get acclimated

## First Week Goals

- Complete all required compliance training
- Set up all your tools and accounts
- Have 1:1s with your team members
- Review team documentation and processes
