# Company Drumbeat

The drumbeat is the rhythm of recurring meetings and reviews that keep us aligned, accountable, and moving toward our goals. These aren't bureaucratic overhead—they're the pulse that ensures we're always aware of where we stand and where we're headed.

---

## Philosophy

We believe in radical transparency, minimal meetings, and strong teamwork. Every recurring event in our drumbeat exists for a reason:

* **Alignment over updates** — We don't meet to share status that can be written down. Status lives in Linear.
* **Async first, synced when needed** — We default to async collaboration (docs, comments, Looms, PRs). We meet to resolve uncertainty, make decisions, and unblock each other.
* **Rhythm over randomness** — Predictable cadences reduce coordination costs and create natural forcing functions.
* **Short and focused** — Most meetings are 30 minutes or less. If it needs more time, it's probably a working session, not a sync.
* **Team outcomes > individual output** — We optimize for shared understanding, shared ownership, and helping each other ship.

---

## Work Drumbeat

Our work rhythm keeps initiatives visible, moving forward, and **collaborative by default** — without turning meetings into status meetings.

### Async project updates (Linear)

**All project status updates happen async in Linear.**
Updates should be posted **as work meaningfully progresses** and **at least once per week** for any active project/initiative.

**What a good weekly update includes:**

* What moved since the last update (links to PRs, docs, decisions)
* What's next (next milestone / next 1–3 steps)
* Risks, unknowns, or blockers
* Explicit **asks** (who/what you need, by when)

**Norms:**

* Prefer commenting on the Linear issue/thread so the context stays centralized.
* Use @mentions for the specific help you need (review, decision, pairing, customer input).
* If you're stuck >24 hours, post an ask immediately — don't wait for a meeting.

### Weekly Work Sync

**Format:**

* **Silent scan (5 min)** — Everyone quickly reviews the Linear updates posted since last week (especially asks/blockers).
* **Initiative spotlight (10 min)** — One initiative owner shares *context*, tradeoffs, and what help is needed (not a timeline of tasks).
* **Blockers & cross-team coordination (10 min)** — Resolve the highest-leverage asks: reviews, pairing time, dependencies, sequencing.
* **Decisions (5 min)** — Make/confirm any calls needed to keep momentum.

**Preparation:**

* Project leads post async updates in Linear **before** the meeting (at least weekly).
* Add agenda items only if they need a decision, a cross-team alignment moment, or real-time unblocking.
* Spotlight rotates weekly and should include a clear ask (reviewers, partner team, customer input, etc.).

## Ad-hoc Working Sessions

Not everything fits into a recurring cadence. For complex work that needs real-time collaboration:

* **Schedule as needed** — Don't wait for the weekly sync if you're blocked.
* **Time-boxed** — Maximum 90 minutes with a clear objective.
* **Collaborative by design** — Invite only the people needed to solve the problem, pair, or make the decision.
* **Documented** — Decisions and action items recorded immediately in the relevant doc/Linear issue.
* **Optional attendance** — Only people who can contribute.

---

## Async Collaboration Expectations

Async collaboration is how we avoid silos:

* **Share early** — Post drafts early in docs/PRs/Linear; invite feedback before things harden.
* **Ask for help explicitly** — Tag the people you need, with a clear request and deadline.
* **Default to review + pairing** — Use PR reviews, design reviews, and pairing blocks to spread context.
* **Make dependencies visible** — Cross-team work should have a clear "owner + partners" and explicit handoffs in Linear.

---

## Async by Default

Remember: the drumbeat exists to supplement async work, not replace it.

Before scheduling a meeting, ask:

* Can this be a Linear update or comment thread?
* Can this be a doc with comments?
* Can this be a Loom video?

If yes to any of the above, do that instead. Save synchronous time for decisions, alignment, and connection.
