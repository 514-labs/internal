# Remote Work Policy

We embrace flexible work arrangements. This policy outlines expectations for remote work.

## Core Hours

While we offer flexibility, we ask that everyone be available during core hours:

- **Monday - Friday**: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM (your local time)
- These hours ensure overlap for meetings and collaboration

## Communication Expectations

- Respond to Slack messages within 4 hours during work hours
- Keep your calendar up to date
- Set your Slack status when unavailable
- Use async communication by default

## Equipment

We provide:

- Laptop (refreshed every 3 years)
- $500 home office stipend
- $100/month internet allowance

## In-Person Requirements

- Quarterly team offsites (company-paid travel)
- Annual company all-hands
- Optional: access to co-working spaces

## Tips for Success

1. **Create a dedicated workspace** - Separate work from personal space
2. **Stick to a routine** - Maintain regular working hours
3. **Take breaks** - Step away from your screen regularly
4. **Over-communicate** - Share your progress and blockers proactively
