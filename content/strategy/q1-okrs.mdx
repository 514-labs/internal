# Q1 2025 OKRs

Our objectives and key results for Q1 2025 (January - March).

## Objective 1: Accelerate Product Velocity

Deliver more value to customers faster.

### Key Results

1. **KR1**: Ship 10 major features (up from 6 in Q4)
2. **KR2**: Reduce average bug fix time from 5 days to 3 days
3. **KR3**: Achieve 95% sprint commitment accuracy

## Objective 2: Strengthen Customer Relationships

Deepen our partnership with existing customers.

### Key Results

1. **KR1**: Conduct QBRs with 100% of enterprise customers
2. **KR2**: Increase NPS from 45 to 50
3. **KR3**: Reduce support ticket volume by 20% through self-service

## Objective 3: Build the Team

Grow and develop our team to support scale.

### Key Results

1. **KR1**: Hire 8 new team members across engineering and sales
2. **KR2**: Complete leadership training for all managers
3. **KR3**: Achieve 90% participation in employee engagement survey

## Tracking

- Weekly: Team leads update progress in Linear
- Bi-weekly: All-hands OKR review
- Monthly: Executive team deep-dive
