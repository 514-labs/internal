# Our Mission

Our mission is to transform the data and analytics stack into something simple, intuitive, and delightful for all developers.

---

## What the World Looks Like If We Succeed

If we accomplish our mission:

- Building with data becomes accessible to every developer, not just specialists.
- Teams move faster with fewer handoffs and less friction.
- The data stack feels like modern software—intuitive, reliable, and easy to work with.
- Organizations unlock more value from their data because more people can build, experiment, and ship with confidence.
