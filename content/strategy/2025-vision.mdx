# 2025 Company Vision

Our strategic vision and priorities for the year ahead.

## Mission

To empower organizations with tools that make work more efficient, transparent, and enjoyable.

## 2025 Theme: Scale with Quality

This year, we're focused on scaling our business while maintaining the quality and attention to detail that got us here.

## Strategic Priorities

### 1. Product Excellence

- Launch Analytics Suite v2.0
- Achieve 99.99% uptime
- Reduce page load time by 50%

### 2. Customer Success

- Achieve 95% customer satisfaction
- Reduce churn to <5% annually
- Expand enterprise customer base by 40%

### 3. Team Growth

- Grow team from 56 to 80
- Maintain high employee NPS (>50)
- Invest in leadership development

### 4. Market Expansion

- Launch in 3 new markets
- Establish partner ecosystem
- Build brand awareness

## Key Metrics

| Metric | Current | Target |
|--------|---------|--------|
| ARR | $5M | $10M |
| Customers | 200 | 400 |
| NPS | 45 | 55 |
| Uptime | 99.9% | 99.99% |

## How We'll Get There

1. **Focus** - Say no to distractions
2. **Execution** - Ship consistently and iterate
3. **Collaboration** - Break down silos
4. **Customer Obsession** - Every decision starts with the customer
