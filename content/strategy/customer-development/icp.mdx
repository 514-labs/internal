# ICP Market Focus

Our ideal customer profile (ICP) framework categorizes potential customers across three tiers based on their fit for our platform and the appropriate go-to-market motion.

---

## Tier Overview

| Tier | Name | Focus |
|------|------|-------|
| **Tier 1** | Core | Target with outbound and sales efforts |
| **Tier 2** | Adjacent | Inbound only |
| **Tier 3** | Self-Serve | Disqualify immediately / self-service |

---

## Qualification Matrix

| Criteria | Tier 1/Core | Tier 2/Adjacent | Tier 3/Self-Serve |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| **Employees** | 100 - 10K | 100 - 10K+ | Under 100 |
| **Industry** | B2B SaaS (external analytics as secondary competency) | Scaled B2C (internal analytics and/or external analytics as core competency) | Any others |
| **Location** | US/Canada | English (or bi-lingual) speaking countries | Non-english |
| **Has developers** | Yes (app team - see User Persona) | Yes (app, platform or data eng) | No |
| **Technology + Signal** | ClickHouse (explorer/adopter) ∥ MooseStack (explorer/adopter) | Other OLAP or scaled OLTP | No OLAP or scaled OLTP |
| **Data Volumes** | 10m+ rows in OLTP, 100k+ customers | 1m+ rows in OLTP, 100k+ customers | No scale |

## How to Use This Framework

1. **Lead Qualification**: Use the tier criteria to quickly qualify or disqualify leads
2. **GTM Motion**: Match the sales motion to the tier—outbound for Tier 1, inbound-only for Tier 2, self-serve for Tier 3
3. **Resource Allocation**: Focus sales and success resources on Tier 1 accounts
4. **Marketing**: Target Tier 1 ICP characteristics in advertising and content
5. **Product-Led Growth**: Build self-serve paths for Tier 3 to convert without sales involvement
