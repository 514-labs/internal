## Objective

Ensure Boreal's product, onboarding, and infrastructure scale with growth — without rewrites, heroics, or bespoke solutions.

## Context

Platform excellence is the foundation that makes PLM and SLG possible:

- **Fast, reliable onboarding** — PLM requires sub-5-minute time-to-value
- **Observable, scalable infrastructure** — Growth can't break the product
- **High engineering velocity** — Ship fast without breaking things
- **One platform, two motions** — PLM and SLG run on the same foundation

This goal focuses on DORA metrics and user-facing performance — the leading indicators that predict whether the platform can support $10M ARR.

## Key Results Rationale

### Core Workflow P95 Latency (200ms)
User-facing performance at scale. The 95th percentile must be under 200ms to maintain the snappy experience developers expect.

### Onboarding Flow Completion Rate (85%)
Measures friction in the self-serve path. If 15%+ of users drop off during onboarding, PLM can't succeed.

### Weekly Deployment Frequency (20 deploys)
Engineering velocity indicator from DORA research. High-performing teams deploy multiple times per day. 20/week indicates healthy velocity.

### Change Failure Rate (5%)
DORA metric for reliability. Elite performers maintain under 5% change failure rate. This ensures deploys don't break production.

### Time to Restore Service (30 minutes)
Operational maturity indicator. When things break (and they will), elite teams restore in under 30 minutes. This maintains user trust.

## Success Criteria

- Onboarding and core workflows are fast, reliable, and observable
- Product decisions favor leverage and repeatability
- Engineering effort compounds over time
- One platform cleanly supports PLM and SLG
