## Objective

Turn activated developer usage into mid-market and enterprise revenue through a focused, low-friction sales motion.

## Context

Sales-Led Growth (SLG) in a product-led company is fundamentally different from traditional enterprise sales:

- **Sales accelerates, doesn't discover** — Developers already know and use the product
- **Product-Qualified Leads (PQLs)** — Usage signals indicate readiness, not form fills
- **Buyer narratives** — ROI, security, and scale matter more than feature demos
- **Land and expand** — Team → org → enterprise expansion path

The SLG motion only works when PLM has done its job. We don't sell to cold accounts.

## Key Results Rationale

### PQL to SQL Conversion Rate (25%)
Quality of the PLM-to-SLG handoff. A 25% conversion rate indicates PQL scoring is working and sales is engaging the right accounts.

### Average Sales Cycle Length (45 days)
Deal velocity for product-led enterprise. Because developers already use the product, cycles should be half traditional enterprise sales (90 days → 45 days).

### Average Contract Value ($75K)
Mid-market ACV target. Supports $10M ARR math: 133 deals at $75K ACV = $10M. Combined with self-serve revenue, this is achievable.

### Expansion Revenue as % of New (30%)
Health of the land-and-expand motion. 30% expansion revenue reduces pressure on new logo acquisition and indicates strong product-market fit.

### Win Rate on PQL-Sourced Deals (40%)
PQLs should close at 2x the rate of cold leads. If PQL win rate isn't significantly higher, the PQL definition needs refinement.

## Success Criteria

- Sales works only with product-qualified accounts
- Buyers see Boreal as infrastructure, not a tool
- Expansion happens naturally through usage growth
- Sales cycle is half of traditional enterprise
