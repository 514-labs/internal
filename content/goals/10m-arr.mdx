## Objective

Build a repeatable growth engine that combines Product-Led Marketing (PLM) for developers and Sales-Led Growth (SLG) for buyers to reach a $10M ARR run rate within 12 months.

## Context

This is the anchor company goal that all other objectives ladder up to. Success requires:

- **Product creates demand** — Developers discover, try, and adopt Boreal through the product itself
- **Partners create trust** — Technical ecosystem partners amplify reach and credibility
- **Sales accelerates revenue** — Activated usage converts to mid-market and enterprise contracts

## Key Results Rationale

### ARR ($10M)
The primary outcome metric. All other KRs are leading indicators that support this target.

### Net Revenue Retention (120%)
Top-quartile SaaS benchmark (Snowflake, Twilio). High NRR indicates product stickiness and expansion potential, reducing reliance on new logo acquisition.

### PLM-to-Paid Conversion (5%)
Industry benchmark for developer self-serve products ranges from 2-7%. At 5% conversion with 50K MAD, this supports the revenue math for $10M ARR.

### Pipeline Coverage (3x)
Standard enterprise sales benchmark. 3x coverage ensures revenue predictability even with normal deal slippage.

### Monthly Active Developers (50K)
Leading indicator for the funnel. Supports the conversion math: 50K × 5% = 2,500 paid accounts, enabling $10M ARR at $4K average.

## Success Criteria

- Repeatable, measurable growth engine (not heroics)
- Balanced contribution from PLM and SLG motions
- Healthy unit economics (LTV:CAC > 3:1)
- Clear path to continued growth beyond $10M
