## Objective

Validate that our core product solves a real problem customers will pay for.

## Context

We are targeting early-stage startups who need better internal tooling and documentation systems. Our goal is to prove that:

1. The problem is real and painful enough to pay for
2. Our solution is compelling compared to alternatives
3. We can acquire customers at a sustainable cost

## Success Criteria

- At least 10 paying customers by end of Q1
- $50K MRR demonstrates willingness to pay
- 40% activation rate shows product delivers value
- Customer interviews inform product roadmap

## Risks

- Market timing during economic uncertainty
- Competition from well-funded alternatives
- Need to balance feature development with sales
