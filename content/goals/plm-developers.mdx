## Objective

Make Boreal easy for developers to find, easy to try, and clearly differentiated — using the product itself as the primary marketing and growth channel.

## Context

Product-Led Marketing (PLM) flips the traditional marketing funnel. Instead of content → demo → trial → value, developers experience value first and upgrade when they hit natural expansion triggers.

The best developer tools (Stripe, Vercel, Supabase) achieve this through:

- Frictionless self-serve onboarding
- Immediate "aha" moments
- Shareable outputs that bring in new users
- Natural upgrade paths that don't interrupt flow

## Key Results Rationale

### Time-to-First-Value (5 minutes)
The most critical PLM metric. Best-in-class developer tools deliver first value in under 5 minutes. Every minute of friction compounds into abandonment.

### Week 1 Activation Rate (40%)
Percentage of signups who complete the core activation action within 7 days. This is the moment developers understand why Boreal exists.

### Self-Serve Upgrade Rate (8%)
Free-to-paid conversion without sales touch. Higher than the 5% company target because PLM-engaged users have stronger intent.

### Organic Signups via Product Sharing (25%)
Measures virality and shareability of outputs. When developers share their work, it becomes natural product marketing.

### 30-Day Retention (50%)
Retention is the multiplier for all acquisition. A 50% 30-day retention rate indicates genuine product-market fit with developers.

## Success Criteria

- Developers can go from zero to first value in under 5 minutes
- The product teaches itself — minimal documentation required
- Sharing is natural and brings new users
- Upgrades feel like unlocking, not paywalling
