## Objective

Drive trusted awareness for both developers and buyers by leveraging technical ecosystem partners for distribution, credibility, and reach.

## Context

Awareness built through partners is qualitatively different from paid or owned awareness:

- **Credibility transfer** — Partners vouch for Boreal in contexts where we have no presence
- **Audience access** — Partners have relationships we can't replicate
- **Trust economics** — Partner endorsement is worth more than self-promotion

This goal has two distinct motions:

### Developer Awareness (PLM-Focused)
- Partner-led developer content
- Co-authored or co-promoted technical material
- Real architectures, workflows, and production use cases
- Direct funnel into self-serve onboarding

### Buyer Awareness (SLG-Focused)
- Enable technical partners' sales teams to introduce Boreal
- Boreal positioned as core infrastructure in partner-led deals
- Awareness driven through credibility and existing buyer trust

## Key Results Rationale

### Partner Co-Created Content (24 pieces)
Two pieces per month cadence. Each piece must feature production use cases and real architectures — not marketing fluff.

### Partner Content to Signup Rate (15%)
Attribution from partner content to trial signup. This ensures content creates action, not just awareness.

### Partner-Sourced Qualified Opportunities (50)
Measures effectiveness of partner sales enablement. These are opportunities introduced by partner sales teams, not co-marketed leads.

### Partner-Influenced Pipeline Value ($5M)
Revenue attribution to partner relationships. At 50% close rate, this represents $2.5M in partner-influenced revenue.

## Success Criteria

- Partners actively recommend Boreal without prompting
- Developer content drives measurable signups
- Partner sales teams include Boreal in their deals
- Awareness always connects to downstream action
