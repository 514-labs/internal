## Objective

Successfully expand into the enterprise market to increase revenue potential and market position.

## Context

Enterprise customers represent our best growth opportunity:

- Higher contract values ($100K+ annually)
- Longer retention and predictable revenue
- Strong references for future sales

## Requirements

### Security & Compliance
- Complete SOC 2 Type II audit
- Implement SSO with SAML 2.0
- Add audit logging and data retention controls

### Enterprise Features
- Role-based access control (RBAC)
- Custom SLAs and support tiers
- API rate limits and usage tracking
- Multi-tenant data isolation

## Success Metrics

A successful Q1 means:
- 5+ qualified enterprise opportunities
- At least 2 closed deals
- $100K average contract value achieved
